Method, maturity and monthly trend

cohort FTD is assigned to signup month; cohort CPA = same-window spend / cohort FTD; ROAS = same-cohort company PNL / spend, shown as a percentage.

Recent cohorts are directional because value can develop later. Use a 90-day PNL review before calling a route efficient.

Web vs app strategy distinction

Web strategy — one campaign, four ad groups

The web route is intent-led. Use one UAE Search acquisition campaign so budget and learning stay controlled, then split message intent by ad group: trade synthetics on MT5, MT5 setup, TradingView bridge, and forex/gold/oil/synthetic indices from one account.

Budget: $6,000/month. Primary KPI: cohort FTD CPA; support KPIs: real-account rate and LP CVR.

App strategy — app campaigns, theme-based assets

The app route is onboarding-led. Google App campaigns do not work like Search ad groups, so mirror the same themes through creative asset groups/ad-set equivalents and judge them by install → signup → real account → FTD quality.

Budget: $4,000/month. Primary KPI: cohort FTD CPA; support KPIs: install-to-signup and real-account rate.
